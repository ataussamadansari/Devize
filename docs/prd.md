# Devize Tech Solutions — Website PRD

**Product:** Devize Tech Solutions (DTS) Official Website
**Type:** Software Service Startup Website
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Planning

---

## 1. Product Overview

Devize Tech Solutions (DTS) is a software service startup providing modern digital products and technology solutions for startups, businesses, institutions, and organizations.

The website will act as:

* Company identity
* Service showcase
* Portfolio
* Lead-generation platform
* Client trust platform
* Technology showcase
* Company information hub
* Contact and project inquiry platform

### Primary Goal

Convert website visitors into potential clients while establishing DTS as a modern, reliable and technology-focused software company.

# 3. Website Goals

### Business Goals

1. Generate qualified leads.

4. Build brand credibility.
